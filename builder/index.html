Letter Stereogram

A poem with a shape hidden in it. Each line of the poem repeats across its row, together with the coloured tiles behind it. Where the shape sits, the repeat is a few letters shorter. Relax your eyes and that shift turns into depth. The left edge always reads cleanly, and the whole poem is printed below the picture.

Look through the screen

      
    
        
  1. Move your face close to the screen, about 20 cm, so everything is blurry.
    2. 
        
  2. Look at the two dots and let your eyes go slack, as if you were staring at a wall far behind the screen. Each dot doubles.
    3. 
        
  3. Wait until the two middle copies drift together and you see three dots, with the middle one solid.
    4. 
        
  4. Keep that, and slowly lean back. Let your gaze sink into the colours without trying to read. The shape rises out of the page.
    5. 
      

    

    

      
Cross your eyes

      
    
        
  1. Sit at your normal distance and hold a fingertip halfway between your eyes and the screen.
    2. 
        
  2. Look at your fingertip. Behind it, the two dots each double.
    3. 
        
  3. Move the finger closer or further until you see three dots, with the middle one solid.
    4. 
        
  4. Hold your eyes there, take the finger away, and look down into the colours. The shape stands out in front.
    5. 
      

    

    

      
If it does not work

      
    
        
  • Switch on Show answer to see where the shape is, then switch it off again.
    • 
        
  • If the two dots are hard to merge, make the Repeat shorter or the letters smaller. A longer repeat fits more of each line but asks more of your eyes.
    • 
        
  • If the words distract you, turn Words down while you look for the shape.
    • 
        
  • Once you see it, raise Pop-out to make the shape stand further out. If it falls apart, lower it.
    • 
        
  • Try a smaller letter size if the dots are far apart, or a larger one on a big screen.
    • 
        
  • If relaxing your eyes does nothing, try Cross eyes. Most people find one of the two ways easy.
