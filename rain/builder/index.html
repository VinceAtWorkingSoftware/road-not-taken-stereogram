Rain Stereogram

Green characters fall down the screen. Every column of rain repeats across each row, and where the hidden text sits the repeat is a little shorter. Relax your eyes and the text floats in front of the rain. In scrolling mode a message glides past, a few letters at a time.

How you view it
Help

Look through the screen

  1. Move your face close to the screen, about 20 cm, so everything is blurry.
  2. Look at the two dots and let your eyes go slack, as if you were staring at a wall far behind the screen. Each dot doubles.
  3. Wait until you see three dots, with the middle one solid.
  4. Keep that, lean back slowly and let your gaze sink into the rain. The letters float in front of it.

Cross your eyes

  1. Hold a fingertip halfway between your eyes and the screen and look at it.
  2. Move the finger until the two dots behind it turn into three dots.
  3. Hold your eyes there, take the finger away and look down into the rain. The letters stand out in front.

If it does not work

  • Press Pause. A still picture is easier to lock onto; press it again once you see the letters.
  • Keep Tiles on. The still tiles behind the rain give your eyes a steady pattern to match.
  • Lower the Scroll speed if the letters smear while they move.
  • Switch on Show answer to see where the letters are.
  • If the dots are hard to merge, make the letters smaller.
  • If relaxing your eyes does nothing, try Cross eyes.
