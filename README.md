# The Road Not Taken, as a stereogram

Robert Frost's poem *The Road Not Taken* (1916), written as a Magic Eye style picture.
Relax your eyes and a forking path appears in 3D, with a signpost at the fork, a tree on the
left and a mountain on the right.

**See it:** https://vinceatworkingsoftware.github.io/road-not-taken-stereogram/

**Build your own:** https://vinceatworkingsoftware.github.io/road-not-taken-stereogram/builder/  
Pick one of five poems or paste your own text, choose a hidden picture (or type a word),
and adjust colours, letter size, depth and viewing method. It also has a full-screen and a
picture-only mode.

## Hello, fellow human

Green characters rain down a black screen while a hidden message scrolls past in 3D, a few letters
at a time. Every frame is a complete stereogram, so the letters stay in depth while the rain falls.

**See it:** https://vinceatworkingsoftware.github.io/road-not-taken-stereogram/rain/

**Build your own:** https://vinceatworkingsoftware.github.io/road-not-taken-stereogram/rain/builder/  
Type any word or message, choose still or scrolling, and adjust letters on screen, scroll speed,
depth, letter size, rain speed and viewing method.

## How to see the picture

1. Move your face close to the screen, so everything is blurry.
2. Let your eyes go slack, as if you were looking at a wall far behind the screen.
3. Slowly lean back without focusing on any letter. The shapes rise out of the page.

## How it works

Every line of the poem repeats across its row, together with the coloured tile behind each
letter. Where the hidden scene stands out, the repeat is a few letters shorter. Each eye
lines up a different copy, and the brain reads the shorter repeat as nearer. The first
repeat on the left is kept flat, so the poem always reads cleanly down the left edge.

Plain HTML and JavaScript, no build step: [`index.html`](index.html) and
[`builder/index.html`](builder/index.html) are the poem picture and its builder,
[`rain/index.html`](rain/index.html) and [`rain/builder/index.html`](rain/builder/index.html)
the rain picture and its builder.

## The poems

Robert Frost's *The Road Not Taken* (1916) and *Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening* (1923) are in
the public domain in the United States. Frost died in 1963, so in countries that protect works for
70 years after the author's death and do not shorten that for American works (Germany among them)
they may stay protected until the end of 2033. William Blake's *The Tyger*, William Wordsworth's
*I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud* and Shakespeare's *Sonnet 18* are in the public domain everywhere.
