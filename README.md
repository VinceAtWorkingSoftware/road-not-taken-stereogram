# The Road Not Taken, as a stereogram

Robert Frost's poem *The Road Not Taken* (1916), written as a Magic Eye style picture.
Relax your eyes and a forking path appears in 3D, with a signpost at the fork, a tree on the
left and a mountain on the right.

**See it:** https://vinceatworkingsoftware.github.io/road-not-taken-stereogram/

## How to see the picture

1. Move your face close to the screen, so everything is blurry.
2. Let your eyes go slack, as if you were looking at a wall far behind the screen.
3. Slowly lean back without focusing on any letter. The shapes rise out of the page.

## How it works

Every line of the poem repeats across its row, together with the coloured tile behind each
letter. Where the hidden scene stands out, the repeat is a few letters shorter. Each eye
lines up a different copy, and the brain reads the shorter repeat as nearer. The first
repeat on the left is kept flat, so the poem always reads cleanly down the left edge.

Everything is in [`index.html`](index.html): plain HTML and JavaScript, no build step.

The poem was published in 1916 and is in the public domain.
